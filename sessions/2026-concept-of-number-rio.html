Introduction to the Concept of Number

Dialectics of the finite and the infinite, the discrete and the continuous, the relative and the absolute

Dates
25–27 August 2026
Place
Bento Prado Jr. Chair, Colégio Brasileiro de Altos Estudos (CBAE), Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
Taught by
Gabriel Catren

In this workshop, we shall propose an introduction to the history and philosophy of the notion of number. To this end, we shall revisit the successive extensions of the notion of number, beginning with the natural numbers ℕ and proceeding through the integers ℤ, the rational numbers ℚ, the real numbers ℝ, and the complex numbers ℂ. We shall then introduce Cantor’s theory of transfinite numbers, including both ordinal numbers, which describe positions in well-ordered sets, and cardinal numbers, which measure the size of collections.

From a philosophical standpoint, we shall use the evolution of the notion of number as a guiding thread for examining a series of conceptual oppositions, namely those between the finite and the infinite, the discrete and the continuous, and the relative and the absolute.
