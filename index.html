L.I.F.E.

Experimental scientific education · since 2018

Conceptualize instead of simplify.

L.I.F.E. is an experimental scientific education project launched by Gabriel Catren in 2018, in the framework of the CNRS international research project Identities, Forces, Quanta. It aims to collectively explore new ways of producing, sharing, and enjoying scientific thought, and, amid the current state of the world, of putting it at the service of life.

Each session is a week-long workshop giving an in-depth introduction to one theory of physics or mathematics, for people who do not necessarily have a university background in science: students and researchers in the humanities, artists, and scientists working beyond their own field. Theories are approached through their conceptual structure, their history and the reading of original texts, without giving up on mathematics.

Most sessions take place at the Performing Arts Forum (PAF), a former convent in Saint-Erme-Outre-et-Ramecourt, France, turned into a residence for artists and researchers, where participants share the cooking and the life of the house. Since 2026 the project also continues at the Bento Prado Jr. Chair of the Colégio Brasileiro de Altos Estudos (UFRJ) in Rio de Janeiro.
